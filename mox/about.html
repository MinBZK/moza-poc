What is MijnOverheid Zakelijk - I am new

On MijnOverheid Zakelijk you can see the information the government holds about your organisation, all in one place.

KVK data

MijnOverheid Zakelijk shows the KVK data of your organisation. This includes your KVK number, the legal form, the registered address and who is authorised to act on behalf of the organisation.

Who is MijnOverheid Zakelijk for?

Conditions for using MijnOverheid Zakelijk:

  • Your organisation is registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (KVK)
  • You are authorised to act on behalf of the organisation
  • You have a DigiD

Do not have a DigiD yet? Apply for one at www.digid.nl.

Logging in to MijnOverheid Zakelijk

You log in with DigiD. The first time you log in, you enter an email address and your preferences once.
